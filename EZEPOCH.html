These frames are simulated from real target positions and approximate proper motions, with six detector bands of 17 channels each. To go live, replace sim() with a loader for SPHEREx cutouts from IRSA; the display, stacking and spectrum code reads only the arrays it produces. Self-hosted copies also accept ?ra=&dec=&fov=.
